Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy covers the "Tiko Tell Studio" application, a private tool that connects to the YouTube Data API to publish videos on the Tiko Tells History YouTube channel. This application is used only by its own operator and is not offered as a service to other users.

What data we access

Tiko Tell Studio requests YouTube upload and manage permissions (the youtube.upload and youtube scopes), used only to upload, update, and organize videos on our own YouTube channel. It does not access any other Google account's data, and it does not read or collect data belonging to viewers of our videos.

How data is stored

OAuth credentials and video metadata are stored locally on the operator's own device. Nothing is uploaded to a third-party server, shared with any other party, or sold. The application displays no advertising and includes no ad or analytics trackers.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Tiko Tell Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You (or anyone whose Google account has granted this application access) can review and revoke that access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
