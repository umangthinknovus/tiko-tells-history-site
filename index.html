Tiko Tells History

Tiko Tells History is a YouTube channel that turns real history into short, dramatized video stories — narrated adventures hosted by our mascot, Tiko the owl, bringing historical figures and events to life for a general audience.

Watch on YouTube

About the "Tiko Tell Studio" app

Tiko Tell Studio is a private, internal tool built to produce and upload videos for the Tiko Tells History channel. It connects to the YouTube Data API solely to upload and manage videos on our own channel.

This is not a public service or product offered to other users — it is used only by the channel's own operator to publish the channel's own content.
